## Effect
<!-- ## 效果 -->

Copy one <img src="/images/tokens/quickplay-icon.png" width="11" height="16" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> effect of a wonder already built by another player. (Effects of transformed tiles will not be copied.)

## Effect Adjustment
<!-- ## 效果调整 -->

- This wonder cannot be build when there is currently no suitable wonder to copy.
<!-- - 当目前没有适合的奇观可以复制时，这个奇观无法被建造。 -->
- If you copy [Glazed Pagoda](wonder_13.md), [Castle](wonder_15.md), etc., you only get the <img src="/images/tokens/quickplay-icon.png" width="11" height="16" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> effect; the subsequent like transformed tile effect cannot be performed.
<!-- - 如果复制的是[琉璃宝塔](wonder_13.md)、[城堡](wonder_15.md)等，只能拿到 <img src="/images/tokens/quickplay-icon.png" width="11" height="16" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> 效果，后续的改造地块操作不能执行。 -->
- [Glacial Debris](wonder_27.md) now has a special ruling due to wording issues; the corresponding laid-down dragon and gaining <img src="/images/tokens/gene-icon.png" width="16" height="16" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> also cannot trigger normally.
<!-- - [冰川残骸](wonder_27.md)现在因文本表述问题有特殊裁定，对应的躺倒龙、获得<img src="/images/tokens/gene-icon.png" width="16" height="16" style="display: inline-block; vertical-align: middle;" /> 也不能正常触发。 -->
