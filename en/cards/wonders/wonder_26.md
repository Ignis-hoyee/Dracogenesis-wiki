## Effect Adjustment
<!-- ## 效果调整 -->

- If a volcano tile covers other transformed tiles, get the resources on those transformed tiles, then remove those transformed tiles. After removal, the resources of the original tiles cannot be gained.
<!-- - 如果火山地块覆盖到了其他改造地块，获得的是改造地块上的资源，然后再移除该改造地块。移除后原本地块的资源不能获得。 -->
- You can also build a nest on a volcano tile.
<!-- - 火山地块也可以建造巢穴。 -->
